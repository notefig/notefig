# Release Notes v0.0.161

- Mermaid diagrams in notes and chat are now drawn as diagrams. Write a ```` ```mermaid ```` block, or ask an agent to diagram something. Select one and press Enter to edit its source.
- Fixed: after Select All (⌘A), arrow keys and clicks sometimes didn't deselect, so ⌘A seemed to do nothing and the next key replaced the whole note.
- Fixed: Select All selected nothing in a note that starts with a checkbox list.
- Fixed: copying a selection that included checkboxes also copied their hidden screen-reader labels.
- Fixed: in a shared workspace, a collaborator's marker sometimes appeared on the paragraph you were typing in instead of theirs.
- Fixed: a paragraph someone was still in could unlock after 10 s of pausing. It now stays locked for up to 30 s while their caret is in it.
- Fixed: when two people started typing into the same paragraph at the same moment, the one shown as holding it could lose their text.
- Pinch-to-zoom is now gentle: one pinch moves one zoom level (the same levels as View › Zoom Level), and your choice is remembered.
- 
- Right-click a file or folder in the sidebar and choose "Reveal in Finder" to see it in Finder ("Show in File Explorer" on Windows).
- When the file tree is sorted by date modified, saving a note (including by sending a prompt) no longer closes the folders you had open.
- In a synced document, you see what someone else is typing as they type it, about a second or two behind, instead of in chunks once they pause. New words are briefly highlighted in their colour.
- Synced branches get far fewer commits: your edits to a paragraph are committed once you move on, not every time you pause.
- Quitting from a terminal, or logging out, now saves and syncs your last edits, as quitting from the menu does.
- Instead of a cursor, each collaborator's name shows on the paragraph they're in.
- Fixed: you could sometimes type into a paragraph someone else was still editing, and their unsaved text could appear twice.
